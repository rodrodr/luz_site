## What happened

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.que_paso.texto -->
Manuel Azaña, President of the Council of Ministers, defends the Statute of Catalonia Bill in the Chamber, in a speech of more than {{puerta.estatuto-1932.umbral}} words.

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.que_paso.2 -->
In the explorer edition (v3) it is the longest spoken intervention in the corpus.

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.que_paso.3 -->
The deposited edition counts it differently, and this gateway shows why.

## What the Diario says

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.diario.cita.azana -->
«Cataluña dice, los catalanes dicen: “quieremos vivir de otra manera dentro del Estado español”. La pretensión es legítima; es legítima porque la autoriza la ley, nada menos que la ley constitucional.»

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.diario.cita.azana.pie -->
Manuel Azaña · V2 {{cita.estatuto.azana.V2|id}} · v3 {{cita.estatuto.azana.v3|id}}

## Two editions, two speakers

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.turnos.titulo -->
Two editions, two speakers

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.turnos.v2 -->
In V2, the speech sits inside a row of more than {{puerta.estatuto-1932.umbral}} words under the name «El Sr. PRESIDENTE».

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.turnos.v2.2 -->
The row begins with the Chair giving the floor and continues with a heading cut off mid-word:

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.turnos.cita.rotulo -->
«El Sr. Presidente del Consejo de Ministros tiene la palabra.» … «El Sr. Presidente del CONSEJO DE MINIS»

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.turnos.cita.rotulo.pie -->
V2 {{cita.estatuto.v2.rotulo.V2|id}}, under the name of Julián Besteiro, who was in the Chair

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.turnos.v3 -->
In v3 there are two rows: {{puerta.estatuto-1932.fila.v3.presidencia.palabras|letra}} words from the Chair and the speech, under Azaña's name.

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.turnos.v3.pie -->
v3 {{cita.estatuto.v2.palabra.v3|id}} and {{cita.estatuto.azana.v3|id}}

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.turnos.efecto -->
So anyone who counts words by speaker in V2 gives this speech to Besteiro.

<!-- sesiones.estatuto-1932.turnos.rango -->
In V2 it is the second-longest row. The longest, by Prieto, contains the «DOCUMENTOS COMPLEMENTARIOS» (supplementary documents) of his speech, which v3 moves to a separate row.
