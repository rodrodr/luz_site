## What happened

<!-- sesiones.cuestion-religiosa-1931.que_paso.texto -->
The Constituent Cortes debate article 24 of the draft Constitution. The Secretary reads out its new wording, which begins:

<!-- sesiones.cuestion-religiosa-1931.que_paso.cita.articulo -->
«Art. 24. Todas las confesiones religiosas serán consideradas como Asociaciones sometidas a una ley especial.»

<!-- sesiones.cuestion-religiosa-1931.que_paso.cita.articulo.pie -->
The Secretary (Ramos)

<!-- sesiones.cuestion-religiosa-1931.que_paso.2 -->
The session begins in the afternoon and lasts all night. Manuel Azaña, then Minister of War, speaks.

<!-- sesiones.cuestion-religiosa-1931.que_paso.3 -->
In the end, the article is approved in a roll-call vote. Alcalá-Zamora and Maura, both members of the provisional Government, are on the list of noes.

<!-- sesiones.cuestion-religiosa-1931.que_paso.4 -->
The next day, the Diario already heads Azaña's rows «Presidente del Gobierno» (Prime Minister).
