## What happened in the Chamber

### The Chamber is constituted (July 1931)

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.1.titulo --> The Chamber is constituted (July 1931)

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.1.a -->
On 14 April 1931, the committee of the allied political forces appointed Niceto Alcalá-Zamora President of the provisional Government of the Republic. That Government summoned single-chamber Constituent Cortes, elected by direct suffrage, for 14 July. The elections were to be held on 28 June.

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.1.b -->
The first session is opened by a Bureau of the eldest members (Mesa de edad), chaired by Narciso Vázquez Lemus (V2 {{fila.I.mesa_edad.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.mesa_edad.v3|id}}). Alcalá-Zamora takes the floor as President of the provisional Government; his speech runs to {{fila.I.alcala_resigna.palabras}} words.

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.1.c -->
He has come to «resignar sus Poderes en fecha próxima» before the Chamber (V2 {{fila.I.alcala_resigna.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.alcala_resigna.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.1.d -->
That same session elects Julián Besteiro interim President, with {{eleccion.besteiro1931.votos}} votes out of {{eleccion.besteiro1931.votantes}} (V2 {{fila.I.besteiro_elegido.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.besteiro_elegido.v3|id}}). The Diario notes that he takes his seat «acogido con una gran ovación» (V2 {{fila.I.besteiro_ovacion.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.besteiro_ovacion.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.1.fuentes -->
[I] Decree of the political Committee, Madrid, 14 April 1931. *Gaceta de Madrid*, no. 105, 15 April 1931, pp. 193–194. · [I] Decrees of the provisional Government on the Constituent Cortes and the elections. *Gaceta de Madrid*, no. 155, 4 June 1931, pp. 1174–1175 and 1181.

### The Constitution (August–December 1931)

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.2.titulo --> The Constitution (August–December 1931)

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.2.a -->
On 27 August, Luis Jiménez de Asúa presents the draft «en nombre de la Comisión», in {{fila.I.asua_proyecto.palabras}} words (V2 {{fila.I.asua_proyecto.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.asua_proyecto.v3|id}}). He defines it thus: «es una Constitución de izquierda».

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.2.b -->
On 1 October, the Chamber approves votes for women.

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.2.c -->
On 13 October, after the debate on the religious question, Article 24 of the draft is carried by {{voto.178-59.si}} votes to {{voto.178-59.no}} (V2 {{fila.I.art24.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.art24.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.2.d -->
The list of those voting no begins with two surnames: Alcalá-Zamora and Maura. They were the President and the Minister of the Interior (Gobernación) of the provisional Government.

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.2.e -->
The next day, Manuel Azaña already speaks as President of the Government. He regrets «la sensible baja que nos ha forzado hoy a este cambio ministerial» (V2 {{fila.I.azana_baja.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.azana_baja.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.2.f -->
On 9 December the Constitution is approved. Of the {{voto.constitucion.prometidos}} deputies who had taken their oath of office, {{voto.constitucion.si}} vote, and all say yes (V2 {{fila.I.constitucion.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.constitucion.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.2.g -->
The next day, the Chamber elects Niceto Alcalá-Zamora President of the Republic, with {{eleccion.presidente1931.votos}} votes out of {{eleccion.presidente1931.votantes}} (V2 {{fila.I.presidente_republica.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.presidente_republica.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.2.fuentes -->
[I] *Gaceta de Madrid*, no. 105, 15 April 1931, p. 194 (appointment of Miguel Maura as Minister of the Interior).

### Governing under the Constitution (1932)

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.3.titulo --> Governing under the Constitution (1932)

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.3.a -->
On 9 March 1932 Azaña speaks, now President of the Council of Ministers (V2 {{fila.I.centro_gravedad.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.centro_gravedad.v3|id}}). He says: «El centro de gravedad de la política de la República española está en el Parlamento, aquí en este salón».

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.3.b -->
On 13 May, José Ortega y Gasset says of the Catalan problem that «es un problema que no se puede resolver, que sólo se puede conllevar» (V2 {{fila.I.conllevar.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.conllevar.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.3.c -->
In the same session Rafael Campaláns answers him, asking for the floor «después de hacerlo el ilustre maestro de todos» (V2 {{fila.I.campalans.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.campalans.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.3.d -->
On 27 May, Azaña defends the Statute of Catalonia in a speech that has its own reading gateway.

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.3.e -->
On 10 August, Azaña comes to the Chamber over «los sucesos acaecidos esta madrugada en Madrid» (V2 {{fila.I.sanjurjo.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.sanjurjo.v3|id}}). He recounts how he learned that General Sanjurjo had appeared in Seville.

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.3.f -->
On 9 September, in a roll-call vote, the Agrarian Reform is carried by {{voto.318-19.si}} votes to {{voto.318-19.no}} (V2 {{fila.I.agraria.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.agraria.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.3.g -->
The same day, the Statute of Catalonia is carried by {{voto.314-24.si}} votes to {{voto.314-24.no}} (V2 {{fila.I.estatuto_voto.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.estatuto_voto.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.3.h -->
They are not exceptions: in this stage, at least {{etapa.I.listas_nominales.filas}} rows of the deposited edition carry a roll-call list, name by name.

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.3.i -->
Those lists are in {{etapa.I.listas_nominales.sesiones}} sessions. They sit inside the text of the rows, not in a column of the dataset.

### Casas Viejas and the wearing down (1933)

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.4.titulo --> Casas Viejas and the wearing down (1933)

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.4.a -->
There was no session in January 1933. The debate on Casas Viejas comes on 1 February, in the first session of the month.

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.4.b -->
The explorer's ready-made debate follows it over {{biblioteca.casas_viejas.sesiones|letra}} sessions, until 16 March (v3).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.4.c -->
From 4 to 6 July, the Chamber debates the new electoral law; it is the ready-made debate «Ley Electoral de 1933» (the 1933 electoral law).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.4.d -->
When the Cortes meet again, on 2 October, Alejandro Lerroux is in government. According to the project metadata, his Government appears before the Chamber in only {{etapa.I.gob.lerroux_1|letra}} sessions.

### The end (October 1933)

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.5.titulo --> The end (October 1933)

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.5.a -->
On 2 October, Lerroux presents his Government to the Chamber (V2 {{fila.I.lerroux_gobierno.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.lerroux_gobierno.v3|id}}). Azaña answers him in {{fila.I.azana_1933.palabras}} words (V2 {{fila.I.azana_1933.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.azana_1933.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.5.b -->
The next day, Lerroux opens his reply like this: «Señores Diputados, los que van a morir os saludan.» (V2 {{fila.I.van_a_morir.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.van_a_morir.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.5.c -->
The Socialist parliamentary minority maintains its motion, defended by Indalecio Prieto. The Government leaves the government bench before the vote (V2 {{fila.I.gobierno_se_retira.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.gobierno_se_retira.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.5.d -->
The motion is carried by {{voto.187-91.si}} votes to {{voto.187-91.no}}, in a roll-call vote (V2 {{fila.I.voto_187.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.voto_187.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.5.e -->
Besteiro closes: «En vista de la declaración del Gobierno, se suspenden las sesiones de Cortes» (V2 {{fila.I.suspenden.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.I.suspenden.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.5.f -->
It was the last session of the Constituent Cortes. A decree of 9 October, signed by Alcalá-Zamora and Diego Martínez Barrio, declared them dissolved.

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.5.g -->
Another decree of the same day called elections for 19 November, with a second round on 3 December.

<!-- cortes.1931.contexto.5.fuentes -->
[I] Decrees of 9 October 1933. *Gaceta de Madrid*, no. 283, 10 October 1933, pp. 251–252.
