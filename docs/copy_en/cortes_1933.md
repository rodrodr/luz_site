## What happened in the Chamber

### The new Chamber (December 1933)

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.1.titulo --> The new Chamber (December 1933)

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.1.a -->
The elections were held on 19 November 1933, with a second round on 3 December. They were the first general elections under Article 36 of the Constitution, which gave women and men an equal vote.

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.1.b -->
On 8 December the session is opened by the eldest member as acting President (Presidente de edad), Honorio Riesgo (V2 {{fila.II.riesgo.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.riesgo.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.1.c -->
Santiago Alba is elected interim President with {{eleccion.alba1933.votos}} votes out of {{eleccion.alba1933.votantes}} (V2 {{fila.II.alba_interino.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.alba_interino.v3|id}}). On 28 December he is proclaimed President of the Chamber (V2 {{fila.II.alba_proclamado.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.alba_proclamado.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.1.d -->
On 19 December, Alejandro Lerroux reads his Government's statement. The first to answer him is José María Gil Robles, on behalf of «la fracción numéricamente más importante de la Cámara» (V2 {{fila.II.gil_robles_fraccion.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.gil_robles_fraccion.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.1.e -->
Who speaks changes. Leaving out the Chair, the right and the far right go from {{ideologia.etapa.I.d_ed.pct|pct1}} of the words in 1931-1933 to {{ideologia.etapa.II.d_ed.pct|pct1}} in this legislature (V2).

[I] Decree of 9 October 1933. *Gaceta de Madrid*, no. 283, 10 October 1933, p. 252. · [I] *Constitución de la República Española*, Article 36. *Gaceta de Madrid*, no. 344, 10 December 1931, p. 1581. · [A] Roberto Villa García, *La República en las urnas*. Madrid: Marcial Pons, 2011.

### From the amnesty to the summer (1934)

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.2.titulo --> From the amnesty to the summer (1934)

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.2.a -->
In April, the Chamber debates the amnesty; the explorer's ready-made debate gathers {{biblioteca.amnistia.sesiones|letra}} sessions, from 11 to 19 April (v3).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.2.b -->
On 20 April, Indalecio Prieto asks: «¿Por qué es inicuo el acto que van a realizar las Cortes al aprobar este proyecto de ley?» (V2 {{fila.II.inicuo.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.inicuo.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.2.c -->
That same day the amnesty is carried by {{voto.265-45.si}} votes to {{voto.265-45.no}}, in a roll-call vote (V2 {{fila.II.amnistia_voto.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.amnistia_voto.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.2.d -->
On 2 May, Ricardo Samper already speaks as President of the Council of Ministers (V2 {{fila.II.samper.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.samper.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.2.e -->
On 31 May, in the middle of José Tomás y Piera's turn, several deputies shout: «Casas Viejas! Casas Viejas! Tiros a la barriga, a la barriga!» (V2 {{fila.II.tiros.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.tiros.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.2.f -->
On 8 June, Alba recalls that requests to prosecute deputies «han de tratarse en sesión secreta» (V2 {{fila.II.sesion_secreta.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.sesion_secreta.v3|id}}). The next row says only: «Luz y taquigrafos.» (V2 {{fila.II.luz.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.luz.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.2.g -->
The Diario attributes it to «JIMÉNEZ FERNÁNDEZ»; the dataset identifies him as Manuel Giménez Fernández. The Chair says it did not see who interrupted (V2 {{fila.II.alba_reglamento.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.alba_reglamento.v3|id}}). The Diario page gathers every time the phrase was heard in the Chamber.

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.2.h -->
On 12 June, the Esquerra minority reads its position and withdraws from the Chamber (V2 {{fila.II.esquerra.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.esquerra.v3|id}}). Samper asks: «¿por qué se retiran los Sres. Diputados de la Esquerra catalana?» (V2 {{fila.II.se_retiran.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.se_retiran.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.2.i -->
After José Antonio Aguirre's turn, the Diario notes: «La minoría vasca se retira del salón» (V2 {{fila.II.vascos.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.vascos.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.2.j -->
On 4 July comes the session of Prieto's pistol. After it, the Chamber does not meet again until 1 October.

### October 1934

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.3.titulo --> October 1934

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.3.a -->
On 1 October Samper speaks: «dijerase que acaba de transcurrir la noche del 4 de Julio en que el Parlamento acordó suspender sus tareas» (V2 {{fila.II.cuatro_julio.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.cuatro_julio.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.3.b -->
Gil Robles asks for «una rectificación»: Samper, he says, «ha puesto de relieve que no es posible seguir por ese camino» (V2 {{fila.II.ese_camino.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.ese_camino.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.3.c -->
The next session, on 9 October, already belongs to the Lerroux IV Government, according to the project metadata.

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.3.d -->
That afternoon, Gil Robles tables a motion. It asks for the sessions to be suspended «hasta completar la pacificación y el imperio de la Ley, interrumpida por la huelga revolucionaria» (V2 {{fila.II.huelga.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.huelga.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.3.e -->
Gil Robles invokes «nuestros compañeros Diputados por Asturias» (V2 {{fila.II.asturias.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.asturias.v3|id}}). The Chamber approves the motion by acclamation.

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.3.f -->
At the end, Lerroux gives cheers for Spain and for the Republic. The Diario notes that «son contestados con unánimes aplausos y aclamaciones» (V2 {{fila.II.vivas.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.vivas.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.3.g -->
In November, the Chamber returns to the events of October and debates the reform of its Standing Orders (Reglamento). Both debates are ready-made in the explorer.

### 1935

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.4.titulo --> 1935: accusations and scandals

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.4.a -->
On 20 March, faced with the proposal to indict him, Azaña speaks. He presents himself as «un presunto acusado y culpable» (V2 {{fila.II.azana_1935.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.azana_1935.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.4.b -->
In both editions, that speech is a single row of {{fila.II.azana_1935.palabras}} words.

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.4.c -->
There is no session in April 1935. Nor in August.

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.4.d -->
On 28 October, the committee that investigated Strauss's complaint reports to the Chamber (V2 {{fila.II.straperlo.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.straperlo.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.4.e -->
Everything turns on an «aparato de juego de salón que ha motivado todo este asunto», called «Straperlo».

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.4.f -->
That day Rafael Salazar Alonso replies: «vengo apesadumbrado por una grave acusación» (V2 {{fila.II.salazar.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.salazar.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.4.g -->
From October to December 1935, «Strauss» appears in {{busqueda.strauss.1935}} interventions and «straperlo» in {{busqueda.straperlo.1935}} (v3).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.4.h -->
On 7 December the committee report on Nombela's complaint is debated (V2 {{fila.II.nombela.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.nombela.v3|id}}). The session totals {{sesion.1935-12-07-275.palabras}} words; only that of 1 July 1936 prints more.

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.4.i -->
On 10 December the crisis of the Chapaprieta Government is read out, and Alba suspends «las sesiones hasta nuevo aviso» (V2 {{fila.II.nuevo_aviso.V2|id}} · v3 {{fila.II.nuevo_aviso.v3|id}}).

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.4.j -->
They never met again. On 7 January 1936, a decree dissolved «las primeras Cortes ordinarias de la República» and called elections for 16 February.

<!-- cortes.1933.contexto.4.fuentes -->
[I] Decree of 7 January 1936. *Gaceta de Madrid*, no. 8, 8 January 1936, pp. 203–204.

## When it met

<!-- cortes.1933.calendario.entrada -->
The legislature left {{etapa.II.meses.sin_sesion|letra}} months without a session: August and September 1934, April and August 1935. The calendar draws them in outline, with no cause.

<!-- cortes.1933.calendario.presidente -->
Santiago Alba is President of the Cortes for {{etapa.II.pres.alba}} of its {{etapa.II.sesiones}} sessions, according to the project metadata.

<!-- cortes.1933.calendario.presidente_otros -->
Cándido Casanueva and Manuel Giménez Fernández are for {{etapa.II.pres.casanueva|letra}} session each.

<!-- cortes.1933.calendario.vice -->
A Vice-President was in the Chair for some stretch in {{etapa.II.vice_ses}} sessions of the stage (V2).

<!-- cortes.1933.calendario.gobiernos -->
The Governments band counts {{etapa.II.gobiernos|letra}}: Martínez Barrio, Lerroux II, III, IV and VI, Samper, and Chapaprieta I and II.

## Who took the floor
