## What happened

<!-- sesiones.casas-viejas-1933.que_paso.texto -->
Between 28 December 1932 and 1 February 1933 the Cortes do not meet: the numbers of the Diario run on without a gap.

<!-- sesiones.casas-viejas-1933.que_paso.2 -->
In its second session of February, Manuel Azaña, President of the Council of Ministers, answers for the events at Casas Viejas.

<!-- sesiones.casas-viejas-1933.que_paso.3 -->
He denies any responsibility on the part of the Government, and the minorities protest. Three weeks later, Martínez Barrio reminds him of his words in the Chamber.

<!-- sesiones.casas-viejas-1933.que_paso.4 -->
The village's name comes back again and again: the search finds it from February 1933 to July 1936.

## What is not there

<!-- sesiones.casas-viejas-1933.no_esta.texto -->
