## What happened

<!-- sesiones.figueres-1939.que_paso.texto -->
It is the last session of the Cortes in Spain. The next will be in Mexico, almost six years later.

<!-- sesiones.figueres-1939.que_paso.2 -->
The corpus does not have the Diario of that night, but an official summary. The motion read out in the session is dated at the «Castillo de Figueras» (castle of Figueres).

<!-- sesiones.figueres-1939.que_paso.3 -->
Diego Martínez Barrio is in the Chair. Juan Negrín, President of the Council of Ministers, makes his statement to the Chamber.

<!-- sesiones.figueres-1939.que_paso.4 -->
The motion is approved in a roll-call vote. The summary prints the list of those who said yes and then the result:

<!-- sesiones.figueres-1939.que_paso.cita.votacion -->
«Han votado afirmativamente los sesenta y dos señores Diputados.»

<!-- sesiones.figueres-1939.que_paso.cita.votacion.pie -->
The Chair (Martínez Barrio)

<!-- sesiones.figueres-1939.diario.cita.negrin -->

Juan Negrín · {{cita.figueres.negrin.palabras}} words
