## What happened

<!-- sesiones.mexico-1945.que_paso.texto -->
In 1945 the Cortes meet in Mexico City. This gateway opens {{puerta.mexico-1945.sesiones|letra}} of their {{fuente.serie.extracto_mexico|letra}} sessions.

<!-- sesiones.mexico-1945.que_paso.2 -->
On 17 August, Diego Martínez Barrio, President of the Cortes, takes the oath as acting President of the Republic. The Vice-President Luis Fernández Clérigo chairs the session.

<!-- sesiones.mexico-1945.que_paso.3 -->
On 7 November, José Giral presents his Government to the Chamber. The next day, the Chamber approves his statement in an ordinary vote. On 9 November, among other business, it debates the Statute of Galicia.

<!-- sesiones.mexico-1945.que_paso.4 -->
The first of the Mexico sessions, that of 10 January 1945, is on the stage page.
