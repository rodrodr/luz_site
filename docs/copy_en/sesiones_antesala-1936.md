## What happened

<!-- sesiones.antesala-1936.que_paso.texto -->
Two sessions on public order, a few weeks before the war. Diego Martínez Barrio is in the Chair for both.

<!-- sesiones.antesala-1936.que_paso.2 -->
On 16 June, Calvo Sotelo clashes with the President of the Council of Ministers, Casares Quiroga. That day, the Diario twice notes words left out of the record on the Chair's orders.

<!-- sesiones.antesala-1936.que_paso.3 -->
On 1 July, the Chair cuts Galarza off mid-sentence and orders that his words be struck from the record.

<!-- sesiones.antesala-1936.que_paso.4 -->
It is the session with the most words in the whole corpus: {{sesion.1936-07-01-54.palabras}} in the deposited edition.

## What is not there
