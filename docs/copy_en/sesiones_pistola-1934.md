## What happened

<!-- sesiones.pistola-1934.que_paso.texto -->
It is night and the Chamber is debating Catalonia. Santiago Alba is in the Chair.

<!-- sesiones.pistola-1934.que_paso.2 -->
While Gil Robles is speaking, two deputies come to blows: Tirado, a Socialist, and Oriol de la Puerta, an Agrarian. The Chair suspends the session for a few minutes.

<!-- sesiones.pistola-1934.que_paso.3 -->
On their return, Prieto gives his version and admits that he drew his pistol. Oriol de la Puerta answers him.

<!-- sesiones.pistola-1934.que_paso.4 -->
It is the last session before the summer: the next one is on {{puerta.pistola-1934.siguiente|fecha_larga}}.

## What is not there

<!-- sesiones.pistola-1934.no_esta.texto -->
The Diario does not say who drew a pistol first. It gives two versions: Prieto's, who drew his after seeing another, and Oriol de la Puerta's.
