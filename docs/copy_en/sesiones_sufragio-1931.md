## What happened

<!-- sesiones.sufragio-1931.que_paso.texto -->
The Constituent Cortes vote on the right to vote in the draft Constitution. The Diario calls it «art. 34 (numeración antigua) del dictamen de la Comisión» (‘art. 34 (old numbering) of the committee report’).

<!-- sesiones.sufragio-1931.que_paso.2 -->
The debate sets two Republican deputies elected for Madrid against each other. Victoria Kent, of the PRRS, asks for women's suffrage to be postponed. Clara Campoamor, of the PRR, asks for it to be recognised now.

<!-- sesiones.sufragio-1931.que_paso.3 -->
Before the article is put to the vote, an ordinary vote rejects a motion of the Socialist group. Then several deputies ask for a roll-call vote, and the article is carried.

<!-- sesiones.sufragio-1931.que_paso.4 -->
Kent votes no; Campoamor, yes. Two months later, Campoamor reads out the adopted article in the Chamber:

<!-- sesiones.sufragio-1931.que_paso.cita.articulo -->
«Los ciudadanos de uno y otro sexo, mayores de veintitrés años, tendrán los mismos derechos electorales…»

<!-- sesiones.sufragio-1931.que_paso.cita.articulo.pie -->
